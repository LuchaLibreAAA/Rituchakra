# India kharif irrigation playbook (bundled RAG)

## Rice (aman / kharif)
- Keep soil moist but avoid standing water immediately before a heavy-rain warning.
- Skip a 20–30 mm irrigation if 3-day rainfall ≥ 25 mm and 0–7 cm soil moisture is already high.
- After flood water recedes, drain fields within 48 hours to protect tillers.

## Vegetables
- Prefer drip or furrow; never irrigate if thunderstorm / very heavy rain is warned for the district.
- Typical skipped irrigation on a 400 m² plot is 2–3 mm equivalent (800–1200 L).

## Cotton / soybean (central India)
- Do not irrigate if soil is near field capacity during monsoon pulses.
- Heat-wave days: irrigate at dawn only.
