# Crop calendar (India, simplified)

## Kharif (June–October)
Rice, maize, cotton, soybean, groundnut, sugarcane (ongoing). Peak water demand: tillering to flowering.

## Rabi (November–April)
Wheat, mustard, gram, potato. Irrigation critical at CRI (wheat ~21 days after sowing) and grain fill.

## Zaid (March–June)
Vegetables, moong. High ET0; prefer evening irrigation.
